# GT63 Words

Version: 1.0

## Purpose

This document defines the official GT63 vocabulary, writing tone and language discipline. Use it with [GT63_EXPERIENCE_BIBLE_EN.md](./GT63_EXPERIENCE_BIBLE_EN.md) and [GT63_DESIGN_RULES.md](./GT63_DESIGN_RULES.md).

GT63 language must feel premium, human, precise and calm. It must never sound like generic AI marketing or a booking engine.

This is Version 1.0 of the GT63 Brand Layer. Future versions may expand the vocabulary, localize terms and refine examples without replacing the core voice.

## Preferred Vocabulary

Use these words when they fit the context:

- Journey
- Journey Book
- Selected Residence
- Travel Advisor
- Traveler
- Explore
- Investment
- Experience
- Signature Proposal
- Destination Story
- Journey Timeline
- Arrival
- Departure
- Curated
- Recommended
- Inspired by
- Prepared for
- Your Journey
- Luxury Collection

## Avoid

Avoid these words in premium client-facing GT63 content unless there is a specific technical or legal reason:

- Offer
- Package
- Cheap
- Discount
- Deal
- Customer
- Client, where Traveler is better
- Document format names in traveler-facing copy
- Booking Engine
- Reservation System
- Price List
- Advertisement
- Generic AI phrases
- Unforgettable memories
- Luxury experience
- Adventure awaits

## Writing Tone

GT63 writing should be:

- calm;
- precise;
- warm;
- editorial;
- confident;
- factual;
- premium without exaggeration.

The tone should feel like a senior travel advisor preparing a private journey, not a marketing engine generating copy.

## Sentence Length

Use short to medium sentences.

Long sentences are allowed only when they carry a clear editorial rhythm. Avoid stacked clauses, generic adjectives and inflated claims.

## Voice

Use active, human language.

Preferred:

"Your journey has been prepared around the selected residence and travel dates."

Avoid:

"A luxury experience has been curated to create unforgettable memories."

## Capitalization

Use capitalization intentionally.

Preferred branded terms:

- GT63
- Journey Book
- Selected Residence
- Travel Advisor
- Journey Timeline

Do not capitalize ordinary words to create artificial importance.

## Typography Usage

Language and typography must work together:

- Destination names may be large and expressive.
- Selected Residence names should be clear and elegant.
- Price should be visible, but not loud.
- Metadata should be small, readable and restrained.
- Avoid all-caps blocks except short labels or controlled brand moments.

## Do / Don't Examples

### Journey Identity

Do:

"Your Journey to Tokyo"

Don't:

"Tokyo Travel Package Document"

### Selected Hotel

Do:

"Selected Residence"

Don't:

"Hotel Option"

### Price

Do:

"Total Investment"

Don't:

"Cheap deal"

### CTA

Do:

"Confirm your selected residence with your Travel Advisor."

Don't:

"Click here to book this package."

### Optional Experiences

Do:

"Optional experiences may be added after availability is confirmed."

Don't:

"Adventure awaits with unforgettable luxury experiences."

### Comparison

Do:

"Comparison of residences"

Don't:

"Hotel price list"

### Missing Data

Do:

"To be confirmed"

Don't:

"Included", when inclusion is not verified.

## Bulgarian Guidance

When writing Bulgarian client-facing GT63 content, keep the same principles:

- use clear, polished Bulgarian;
- avoid internal workflow terms;
- avoid cheap promotional language;
- keep the selected journey central;
- never invent unsupported claims.

Preferred Bulgarian equivalents:

- Пътуване
- Избрана резиденция
- Пътешественик
- Вашето пътуване
- Инвестиция
- Хронология на пътуването
- Подготвено за
- Препоръчано
- За потвърждение

Avoid:

- евтино;
- сделка;
- промоция;
- document format names, when speaking to a traveler;
- резервационна система;
- незабравими спомени;
- луксозно преживяване, when used as generic filler.

## Final Language Rule

If a sentence could appear in any generic AI travel brochure, it does not belong in GT63.

GT63 words must make the journey feel specific, cared for and true.
